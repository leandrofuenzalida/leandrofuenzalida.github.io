Chile in the Frontier of AI

Chile-California Council

Visual identity design and graphic pieces for Chile in the frontier of AI, a technological mission that brought together over 40 Chilean leaders from public, academic, business and tech sectors to Silicon Valley.

The challenge was to create a contemporary visual language capable of representing the link between Chile and the global AI ecosystem, accompanying an agenda of meetings with organizations such as Stanford University, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Microsoft and NVIDIA.

The graphic system aimed to balance technology, innovation and institutional representation, developing pieces to communicate the mission before, during and after the tour in California between August 22-28, 2026.
