Conceptual Brand Development + Webflow Development for Fintoc

Fintoc

Brand creation and landing page for Shockpay, a conceptual project developed as part of an application to Fintoc, exploring the idea of transforming a fintech into an energy drink.

The challenge was to develop the concept from scratch, creating the visual identity, packaging, mockups, and graphic language of the brand. I also built a small design system with tokens, components, and assets to maintain visual consistency during development.

I designed the digital experience in Figma and subsequently implemented the landing page in Webflow, developing a responsive experience that connected the brand concept with an attractive and coherent digital proposal.
